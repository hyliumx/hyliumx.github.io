I am a undergraduate student majoring Computer Science at UC San Diego (expected June 2028). I am interested in robot learning and machine learning algorithms, especially imitation learning and reinforcement learning, and in how robots can learn from demonstrations and human video.

I am currently building my foundations in machine learning and RL. I am working through public course materials (Stanford cs231n and Berkeley cs285), implementing baselines in PyTorch, and reading recent robot learning papers. I am looking for opportunities to do research in a robot learning lab.

Before returning to school, I founded and ran a data collection company in Beijing for five years. We delivered image, video, and speech data projects for AI clients in more than nine countries. I closed the company in 2022 and went back to college to study computer science formally.

Outside of research interests, I work in C++, Rust, Java, and Python.

You can find my [CV here](/files/CV_Liam_Xie.pdf) and my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/hyliumx).
